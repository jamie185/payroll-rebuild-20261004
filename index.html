Still open:
spares.

The one you called out. A trainer leaves a late-cancelled session in (paid, fair), then also does a makeup. Today nothing connects the two, so the client's spare count drifts.

The proposed approach

  1. Trainer says what each session wasOn the Friday sign-off: regular, makeup or spare used. One tap per session.
  2. Confidence checks the claimA makeup with no workout, no message and no booking trail scores low and gets asked.
  3. A spare ledger per clientLate cancels kept in add a credit used. Makeups draw one down. Paid sessions are compared with what the client pays for.
  4. Double-count alertA late cancel left in and a makeup within 14 days for the same client: flagged on the card, with the two sessions side by side.
  5. Trainer confirms the count monthlyExact match with a high-confidence ledger becomes the new anchor. Otherwise you approve it in the desk.
